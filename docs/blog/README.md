## What it is

The experience and the struggles behind lifting, running and building Qala. First person, bylined berkley, public. The interesting material is the parts that did not work: the decisions that got reverted, the things that turned out to be assumptions, the six weeks that made the fatigue model behave.

Not a product announcement feed, and not the app's documentation. The app has its own docs and they are written in a different voice entirely.

## Rules

The owner's prose rules from `docs/PLAN.md` section 2 apply: no em dashes, no "Co-Authored-By" or similar trailers, plain colleague voice. Add two more:

- Nothing reads as if an agent wrote it. No "it's worth noting that", no symmetric paragraphs, no summarising the post at the end.
- Claims that come from research carry the same discipline as the rest of this repo: a tag and a source, or they do not appear. "I looked it up" is not a source.
