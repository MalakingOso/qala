Dark pass: rail strip, menu and notch seam

The spec has no dark values for the strip, the menu or the seam; this page proposes three sets and puts the light values next to them. Every frame forces its own theme, so light and dark show side by side whatever the page theme is. Dark tokens are the DESIGN.md section 2 ones: ground #0b1020, surface #121a33, ink #e8ecf6, teal #2dd4bf for done, ember #fb923c for now, and hard shadows in #2a3350 because black shadows vanish on the dark ground. Frames are real Today screens with the notched cards, the glass preview bar and, in row two, the menu open on Run.

What the dark pass found in the light spec. (1) The seam stroke in the prototype is rgba(15,21,42,.24), darker than the card border (.16); the M3 plan says the seam uses the card's own border, so the light column here uses .16. (2) The selected-row text and the selected-dot halo are hard-coded white, which would stay white on a light row in dark, so they become tokens. (3) The scrim is ink at .22 in light; ink is light in dark, so the same token would lighten the screen. Dark needs a near-black scrim. (4) A later dot's border is about 1.7:1 against the strip in light; the dark sets below hold about 3:1, and light should follow.
