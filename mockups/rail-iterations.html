Four rails to compare

A · Dots is the current direction: six tiny dots in a frosted strip hugging the left edge, vertically centered (menu demo in rail-cutline.html). B · Angle is the full-height cut with one straight diagonal seam. C · Micro shrinks the full-height rail to a dot strip. D · Tabs drops the rail panel entirely: notebook dividers jutting from the edge. Tap any node or tab to preview that stage; swipe up and down on the rail to step through them.

A · Dots. Tiny frosted strip fixed mid-screen; boxes deform around it in a square notch as they scroll. Full demo + behavior spec in rail-cutline.

B · Angled seam. Same idea, one straight diagonal: narrow at top, wide at the bottom. Sharper, simpler geometry.

C · Micro strip. A 36px dot strip: status colors only, no numbers, smallest possible cut. Expands like A on swipe.

D · Divider tabs. No panel at all: six tabs jut from the edge like notebook dividers. Nothing to open or close.
