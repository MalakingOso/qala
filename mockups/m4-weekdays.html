Training weekdays

Seven Mon to Sun toggles, default Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat, with one line saying what it changes. The setting decides which weekdays are lift days; every other weekday is a rest day (three rail stages, the four fixed check-in muscles). The program's days follow in order, so changing it moves upcoming days and leaves logged days alone. The picker lives in Settings under the Training status group, and at least one day stays on. Cells and toggles are tappable.

In Settings

Row one is the default. Row two is the edge: only Saturday is left, so its control is held on and says why.
